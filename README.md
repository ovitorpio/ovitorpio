# Hi, I'm Vitor Pio

Selling in another country shouldn't require a whole new financial setup.

I'm the Founder & CEO of [TroqPay](https://troqpay.com), an intelligent global business account. It brings local payments, international collections, supplier payments and reconciliation into one platform. With **Elisa**, our AI, businesses turn their own data into insights about where to grow.

## Who we build TroqPay for

- **SaaS & platforms** - automate billing, access and reconciliation.
- **E-commerce & marketplaces** - sell with local payment methods and run one operation.
- **Importers & exporters** - collect, pay and reconcile across markets.
- **Digital businesses** - start with payment links and automate at your own pace.
- **Professional services** - bill international clients without manual follow-up.
- **Partners** - agencies and advisors who bring the Global Account to their clients.

## What I'm working on

- Building **TroqPay** end to end: payment flows (Pix, payment links, APIs and webhooks, USD and EUR virtual accounts, stablecoin settlement), banking integrations (ACH, Wire, SWIFT and SEPA), security and operations.
- Expanding TroqPay with native **USDC on Stellar**, backed by a $60,000 Build Award from the Stellar Community Fund #44.
- **Elisa**, which applies AI to payment and operational data so businesses know where to sell more.
- A developer path that starts with payment links and grows into **API, SDK and webhooks**, plus an `llms.txt` and an MCP plugin for AI tools.

## Background

My background spans engineering, security and business. Before TroqPay, I founded **PrivacyMap**, a security consultancy where I led penetration tests and security assessments for web, Android and iOS applications. Earlier, I worked on **Samsung R&D** cybersecurity projects at SiDi, covering smartphones, smartwatches, embedded systems and web applications.

I hold an **MSc in Computer Science** from Fluminense Federal University (UFF), where I researched quantum computing applied to cryptography, implementing and evaluating Shor's algorithm on real IBM quantum hardware.
