# Hi, I'm Vitor Pio

I'm the Founder & CEO of [TroqPay](https://troqpay.com), where I build payment infrastructure to help businesses sell locally, receive globally, and grow without rebuilding their financial operations country by country.

My work sits at the intersection of **payments, cybersecurity, product, and emerging technologies**. I enjoy turning complex systems into practical products that people can understand and trust.

## What I'm working on

- Building **TroqPay**, from product and technology to security and operations.
- Exploring how **AI** can make sales and financial operations more useful and accessible.
- Researching **quantum computing applied to cryptography**, with a focus on Shor's order-finding routine on real hardware.
- Sharing what I learn about technology, payments, security, and entrepreneurship on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ovitorpio).

## Selected work

- **[TroqPay](https://troqpay.com)** - payment infrastructure for businesses expanding beyond borders.
- **[TroqPay JavaScript/TypeScript SDK](https://github.com/troqpay/sdk)** - the official server-side SDK for building checkout and payment experiences with the TroqPay API.
- **[TroqPay agent plugin](https://github.com/troqpay/agent-plugin)** - an open-source MCP integration that connects TroqPay payment tools to Codex, Claude, and other compatible AI clients.
- **[Factoring Some Integers Using Shor's Algorithm on Real Quantum Hardware](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-9793578/v1)** - co-authored research preprint on explicit modular exponentiation and depth-induced degradation on IBM quantum hardware.
- **[Research code and data](https://github.com/gdhfontoura/fermat-moduli-factoring-circuits)** - reproducible notebooks, circuits, and experimental results from the quantum research.
- **[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@ovitorpio)** - conversations and lessons from building at the intersection of technology and business.
